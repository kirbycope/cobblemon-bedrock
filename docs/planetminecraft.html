Cobblemon Bedrock

Catch, battle, train and ride Pokémon in Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Pick a starter from any region, fill your Pokédex, build a team of six, and take on wild Pokémon, trainers and your friends.

This is a Bedrock Edition add-on port of Cobblemon, the open-source Pokémon mod for Java Edition by the Cobblemon team. The Pokémon models, animations, textures, sounds and data all come from Cobblemon itself, converted by a script, so every Pokémon looks, moves and sounds the way it does in the mod.

📥 Installation

  1. Download cobblemon-bedrock.mcaddon from the latest release.
  2. Double-click the .mcaddon file to import both packs into Minecraft Bedrock.
  3. In a world's settings, add the Cobblemon behavior pack (it brings its resource pack with it) and load the world.

No experiments are needed. Made for Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.52 and tested on a dedicated server.

🎮 How It Works

When you first join, the starter screen opens: choose a region, then your partner Pokémon. You also get two books signed by Kirbycope, a README with every control and a Getting Started guide, and the Party item. Your Pokémon travel with you in their Poké Balls and show down the left of your screen. Send one out with the Party item, battle wild Pokémon to level up, catch more with Poké Balls crafted from apricorns, and keep the rest in a PC.

Core Features

  • 🐾 904 Pokémon: every species Cobblemon has, with their regional forms, gendered and shiny looks, cries and animations, spawning in the biomes and at the times of day Cobblemon gives them.
  • ⚔️ Turn-based Battles: Cobblemon's battle screen with 696 moves, abilities, stat stages, statuses, weather, held items and each move's animation, sounds and particles. Fight, switch, use items, catch or run.
  • 🎒 Party and PC: six Pokémon in their balls with the party overlay, a PC with 40 boxes of 30, and pastures where boxed Pokémon roam.
  • 🎯 Catching: all 49 Poké Balls with their own catch rules, and the throw, shake and capture of the mod.
  • ✨ Evolution: by level, by the 52 evolution items and by trade, with the evolution animation.
  • 💪 Training: natures, IVs and EVs, 335 TMs and the TM Machine, held items, friendship, candies and 168 marks.
  • 🐉 Riding: 93 species you can ride, 43 of them flying, with stamina and the ride controls on screen.
  • 📖 Pokédex and Scanner: register what you see and catch, and scan Pokémon in the world.
  • 🤝 Multiplayer: trade Pokémon with other players and challenge them to battles.
  • 🌿 Living World: wild Pokémon move in herds, flee when hurt, and some fight back.
  • 🎣 Berries, Apricorns, Fossils and Fishing: 70 berry bushes, seven colours of apricorn tree, fossils revived in a Restoration Tank, and Poké Rod fishing with 48 rods and bait.
  • 🍲 Cooking: the Campfire Pot with Cobblemon's 117 recipes, seasonings and Aprijuices.
  • 🏛️ Structures: Cobblemon's ruins and other generated structures, with their loot.

💡 Good to Know

  • The battle music is not in the download. Battles play without it.
  • Bedrock add-ons cannot add new keys, so the Party item takes the place of Cobblemon's send-out and party keys.
  • Battles are single battles, one Pokémon a side.
  • The menus show rendered portraits of each Pokémon rather than live 3D models.
  • The port follows the mod closely and is still growing. The README on GitHub lists what is carried over and what is not yet.
